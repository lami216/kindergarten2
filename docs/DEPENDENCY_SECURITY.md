## Cause and minimal supported resolution

- [GHSA-78g4-xqwv-q3g8](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-78g4-xqwv-q3g8)
  affects Pillow wheels >=11.1.0,<13.0.0, including 12.3.0. Bundled OpenJPEG
  can perform an out-of-bounds write while decoding untrusted JPEG 2000.
  Patching the host library alone does not repair bundled wheels. The advisory
  identifies 13.0.0 as patched. Application decoder restriction is additional
  protection, not a substitute for upgrading the package.
- [PYSEC-2026-4320](https://osv.dev/vulnerability/PYSEC-2026-4320) concerns
  compromised emSign intermediates/trust roots and on-path TLS interception.
  Its machine-readable affected range includes 2026.10.18 and fixes at
  2026.10.19. Its narrative says .18 removed all emSign roots and .19 restored
  unaffected roots. We follow the published fixed version and do not suppress
  the alert on the basis of that narrative distinction.
- Read official PyPI metadata for **every stable release** from Streamlit
  1.61.0 through 1.66.1. 1.61.1, 1.62.0, 1.63.0, 1.64.0, 1.65.0 and 1.66.0
  all require `pillow<13`. **1.66.1** is the first non-yanked stable release
  permitting `pillow>=9.2.0,<14`, and supports Python >=3.11 including 3.12.
  Sources: [old metadata](https://pypi.org/pypi/streamlit/1.61.0/json),
  [1.66.0](https://pypi.org/pypi/streamlit/1.66.0/json),
  [selected metadata](https://pypi.org/pypi/streamlit/1.66.1/json).
